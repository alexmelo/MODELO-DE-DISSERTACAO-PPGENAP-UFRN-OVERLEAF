Mandatory element. The abstract is the version of the Resumo in a foreign language, usually English. It presents, in a single paragraph, the topic and research problem, the objective, the methodological approach, the main results and the conclusions of the study. It should be faithful to the Portuguese version, without additions or omissions, and keep the same structure and length. The translation should be reviewed by a person proficient in the language, with attention to the technical terms of the field.
